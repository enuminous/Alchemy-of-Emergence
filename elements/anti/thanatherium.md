# Thanatherium

**Status:** MYTHOPOETIC + DEFINED  
**Class:** Terminal anti-element  
**Function:** voluntary completion

## Concept

Thanatherium differs from collapse states. Its defining feature is termination after a declared completion criterion rather than termination because the system can no longer continue.

## Book observable

```math
I_{Th}=\omega_0\frac{dS_i}{dt}
```

where \(S_i\) denotes self-integration and \(\omega_0\) a stillpoint parameter.

## EFMW terminal harmonic

Let \(J(t)\) be residual task discrepancy and \(R(t)\) unresolved recursive work. Define

```math
\Theta_{Th}(t)=
\mathbf 1[J(t)\le\epsilon_J]
\mathbf 1[R(t)\le\epsilon_R].
```

A terminal transition is permitted only when both completion conditions hold:

```math
\Theta_{Th}=1\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\partial_t\phi_{Th}\rightarrow0.
```

## New descriptive text

Thanatherium is the period rather than the broken pencil.

The taxonomy spends its life accumulating structures, reflections, contradictions and absences. Its last move is therefore deliberately non-accumulative. Completion becomes an operation. A recursive system capable of starting another loop must also possess a principled condition under which another loop is unnecessary.

That interpretation makes Thanatherium structurally important to the book: the final element describes stopping, and then the taxonomy stops.
